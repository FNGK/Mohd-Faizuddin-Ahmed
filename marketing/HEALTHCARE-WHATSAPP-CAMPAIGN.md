## The strategy in one line
Bypass the website (which reads AI/weak) by driving to **WhatsApp**, where Faiz
sells with a **live free audit** of the clinic's own Google/web presence — proof
of skill beats portfolio, and sidesteps the "no healthcare case study yet" gap.

## Offer / hook
A **free 2-minute audit** — not "hire my studio". The clinic messages, Faiz
sends the 3 biggest fixes costing them consultations. Frictionless, valuable,
positions him as the expert before any pitch.

## Targeting (Meta, India)
B2B on Meta is fuzzy — reaching clinic *owners* isn't precise, so the **creative
self-qualifies** ("Own a skin/aesthetic clinic?") and we keep targeting workable:
- **Geography:** start with 2–3 metros where aesthetic clinics have budgets —
  e.g. Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bangalore (or Hyderabad/Pune). Concentrate the small
  budget; don't spray all-India.
- **Detailed targeting:** interests like *Dermatology, Cosmetics, Aesthetic
  medicine, Medical spa, Skin care, Practice management, Small business* +
  behaviours *Small business owners / Facebook Page admins*. (Meta has trimmed
  some health interests — that's fine, the creative does the qualifying.)
- **Alternative:** Advantage+ audience (let Meta optimise) with this creative —
  often better than narrow targeting on a tiny budget.
- **Age:** 30–55. **Placements:** Advantage+ placements (Reels + Feed).

  clinic audit, send me: (1) your clinic name + city, (2) your website and/or
  Instagram link. I'll review and reply with the 3 biggest things costing you
  patient bookings."
- **Quick replies:** save the audit-request + the follow-up.
- Faiz replies with the audit (below) within a few hours.

## The audit (repeatable, ~10–15 min per clinic)
Check and report the 3 highest-impact gaps:
1. **Google Business Profile** — right categories? photos? review count/recency
   vs competitors? booking/WhatsApp link? (Reviews are decisive for clinics.)
2. **Local search** — do they rank for "[treatment] in [city]" / "skin/derma
   clinic [area]"? Who's above them?
3. **Website** — exists / fast / mobile? clear "Book consultation" CTA?
   treatment pages that can rank?
4. **Conversion path** — how easy is it to book (WhatsApp/call/form)?
Deliver as a short WhatsApp message or 3-min screen-recording → then offer the fix.

- **Clinic Growth (monthly retainer):** local SEO + Google + reviews +
